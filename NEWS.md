# agriTradeRisk 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN submission.
* Concentration (`export_concentration()`), competitiveness
  (`competitiveness_index()`, `cms_decomposition()`), price transmission
  (`price_transmission()`), currency risk (`exchange_rate_exposure()`),
  entry risk (`market_entry_risk()`) and destination portfolio
  optimisation (`market_diversification()`).
* Simulated example data: `hort_exports`, `trade_totals`, `fx_rates`,
  `grape_prices`.
